# Strategy 5: News-Based Problem Extraction

## 🎯 Core Strategy
Extract problems that can be solved with digital solutions by analyzing content from Nigerian news websites.

## Process

### ChatGPT Prompt Setup
**IMPORTANT**: Use both **ACCESS LINK** and **LINK READER** ChatGPT plugins. The prompt should not be too long for best ChatGPT results. Content copied from source website shouldn't be too long in order to get best ChatGPT results.

### Prompt 1a
**Give me problems that can be solved with digital solutions (web apps and others), based on content on nigerian news websites today. Output should have "With the mention of"**
