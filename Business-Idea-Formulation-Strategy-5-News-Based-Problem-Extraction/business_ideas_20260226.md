## Prompt 1a — Problems (with the mention of the problem)

1. **With the mention of the pivot of Nigerian exporters to China amid US uncertainty**, many export‑oriented firms lack clear tools to compare market risks, trade finance, and logistics between destinations, creating confusion and potential losses for SMEs and large exporters.
2. **With the mention of the “inflation paradox” where a food‑basket state starves while neighbours feast**, local producers and policymakers lack granular, real‑time data and coordination tools to match supply with demand across regions, leading to waste in some areas and shortages in others.
3. **With the mention of concerns that the stock market rally may be a brewing bubble**, retail investors and even professionals lack accessible risk dashboards and scenario tools that translate complex macro/market indicators into simple guidance, risking over‑exposure and wealth destruction.
4. **With the mention of the naira paring gains as the CBN buys dollars to tame the rally**, businesses and individuals struggle to understand FX policy moves and their impact on pricing, contracts, and savings, making planning difficult for importers, exporters, and savers.
5. **With the mention of affordable housing shortages stalling youth homeownership dreams**, young Nigerians lack transparent, digital pathways to discover, qualify for, and finance housing options matched to their income and informal earning patterns.
6. **With the mention of a $1tn economy being at risk due to early childhood crisis**, there is a lack of integrated tools for tracking, funding, and evaluating early‑childhood interventions across states, leaving parents, NGOs, and governments without a shared evidence base.
7. **With the mention of rising cloud and AI infrastructure demand and the N20bn bond for it**, many SMEs and public agencies do not have clear decision tools to choose between local and global providers, migrate safely to the cloud, and model AI‑driven ROI.
8. **With the mention of NIMR’s warning on cervical cancer and antimicrobial resistance**, citizens and health providers lack user‑friendly platforms that translate research into personalised prevention plans, reminders, and facility recommendations.
9. **With the mention of daily blackouts in Benin due to TCN upgrades**, affected residents and businesses lack clear outage schedules, impact maps, and mitigation planning tools (e.g. load‑sharing, generator/solar scheduling), making resilience planning hard.
10. **With the mention of Nigeria’s labour market crisis behind Africa’s demographic growth**, job seekers, employers, and policymakers lack integrated labour‑market intelligence and skill‑gap dashboards that inform training, hiring, and policy at scale.
