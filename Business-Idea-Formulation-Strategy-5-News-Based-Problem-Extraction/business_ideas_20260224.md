## Prompt 1a — Problems (with the mention of the source)

1. **With the mention of The Guardian:** Nigerians are **losing trust in the electoral process**, as highlighted by CISLAC and TMG, which undermines democratic legitimacy and voter participation. This is a problem for citizens, civil society, and policymakers trying to run credible elections.
2. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Rising conflict deaths and communal crises** (e.g. killings in Plateau communities, Zamfara attacks) show that many communities lack timely, localised security and early‑warning information. This endangers residents, local businesses, and authorities.
3. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Dangerous road travel conditions** leading to tragedies like the LAUTECH students’ fatal crash reveal gaps in road‑safety information, vehicle/driver standards, and journey risk alerts for students and commuters.
4. **With the mention of The Guardian:** The push for **renewable energy and stable power** (Dangote Refinery logistics, KEDCO digital payments, renewable energy credits, energy security debates) shows that many businesses and households lack simple tools to understand, choose, and finance reliable energy options.
5. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Education sector reforms and scholarships** (global scholarships, new universities, budget commitments) are scattered across many sources, leaving students and parents without a centralised way to discover, compare, and track opportunities.
6. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Stablecoin and digital‑asset adoption nearing 80% among users** in Nigeria and South Africa creates both opportunities and risks; many everyday users and SMEs lack clear guidance on safe use, regulation, and tax implications.
7. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Digital payments transforming community nightlife** and local commerce show that many small merchants still struggle with onboarding, reconciliation, and understanding the impact of digital payments on their business metrics.
8. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Health challenges** (hospital negligence, cholera prevention, climate‑related health concerns) indicate that residents often lack accessible, localised health‑risk alerts and simple guidance on prevention and where to find quality care.
9. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Climate and energy articles** (climate‑related health concerns, energy policy, renewable initiatives) highlight the need for tools that turn complex policy and technical information into actionable options for ordinary citizens, SMEs, and local governments.
10. **With the mention of The Guardian:** **Tech education and scholarships for 1,000+ students** show high interest in tech careers, but there’s a lack of structured, ongoing support that tracks progress from scholarship to skills to employment.
