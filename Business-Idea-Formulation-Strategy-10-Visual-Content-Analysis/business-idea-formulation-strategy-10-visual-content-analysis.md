# Strategy 10: Visual Content Analysis

## 🎯 Core Strategy
Generate business ideas and IT solutions by analyzing visual content (real estate projects, buildings, etc.) using ChatGPT Vision to identify opportunities for architects, builders, and project developers.

## Process

### Step 1: Prepare Visual Content
Insert a picture of a place, for example:
- Real estate project
- Uncompleted building
- [Example: Instagram photo of a real estate project](https://www.instagram.com/p/C3AAn0XIYqx/?igsh=ZTZjb2kyOGt4NGQw)

### Prompt Variants (Choose one or use all three)
Before proceeding, you may want to open another window to enter 2 variants of this prompt:

1. **Standard Prompt**: Give me business ideas/solutions that can be proposed to the architects or the builders of this uncompleted building [or project].

2. **IT Solutions Variant**: Give me business ideas/IT solutions that can be proposed to the architects or the builders of this project.

3. **Sales Expediting Variant**: Give me business ideas/IT solutions that can be proposed to the architects or the builders of this project to expedite the sales of the project.
