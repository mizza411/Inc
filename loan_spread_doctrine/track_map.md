# Track map

Pointers only. **Do not edit** FIR, Tegrid, gadget Python, Abuja research files, launchers, or main `task.md` for this doctrine. Finding a spread is **operator work**; those tracks are not loan products.

---

## Gadget / supplier float

**Path (cite, do not change):** `Strategy-1-Business-Variation/gadget-business/gadget-business.md` and the gadget-business-automation folder beside it.

**Which variant:** **Supplier float** — goods (or a PO) now, pay the supplier later; customer pays cash or faster than you pay. That is the closed loop **without** a bank: **C** is often ~0% if you stay inside terms; **R** is markup minus logistics and dead stock.

**When it matches this doctrine:** you can name the pay-supplier date and the collect-from-customer date, and cash arrives first (or you still cover **C** if it does not).

**When it does not:** buying stock on a **salary loan**, or holding inventory past terms until penalties become the real **C**.

## Abuja leveraged buy-to-hold

**Path (cite, do not change):** `Abuja-Real-Estate-Research/abuja-real-estate-profitable-sub-niches.md` — section **1.3 Financing environment**. That note already says cash-rich investors and **rental-income** models outperform **leveraged buy-to-hold** in Abuja **unless** NHF/FMBN-eligible. Commercial mortgages are cited around **15–25%**; concessional around **7–10%** if you qualify; suburban gross yields often **~10–15%**.

**Which variant:** usually **do-not** for commercial-mortgage buy-to-hold (negative carry). **Cheap bucket** only if you **actually** qualify and the **cash** rent still beats **that** **C** after voids and repairs. **Invert** / services (agency, management, title-adjacent work) sit on the fee side, not on a 25% mortgage.

**When it matches this doctrine:** short **bridge** or assignment with a dated exit in cash — not a 20-year hope.

## Finding R > C vs FIR and Tegrid

**FIR** (`field_intelligence_radar/`) and **FIR-ABUJA-MON-1** visits are how you **find** paying problems in the field. That is hunt work. It is not a loan feature and must not grow a “borrow” button.

**Tegrid** (`tegrid_re_gtm/`) is software for developers/agencies (listings, clients, ops). That is selling a tool to people who already run deals. It is not “take a facility inside Tegrid.”

**Which variant:** those tracks help you **see** where a spread might exist (operator seat). They do **not** replace the spread test. Do not add lending, BVN capture, or rate scraping to them for this doctrine.

## Which variant fits which track

| Inc track | Default variant from this doctrine | Usually avoid |
|-----------|--------------------------------------|---------------|
| Gadget sourcing / automation | **Supplier float**; recycle after each stock turn | Bank loan for slow gadgets; consumer loans |
| Abuja RE research (existing md) | **Cheap bucket** only if eligible; else cash / services; short **bridge** if dated | Commercial-mortgage buy-to-hold vs 10–15% gross yield |
| FIR visits / monetization | Hunt for **R** (problems people pay for); then pick a variant | Treating visits as a reason to draw a personal loan |
| Tegrid GTM | **Invert** only in the sense of *selling software* (they pay you); not a loan inside the app | Facility product, Hub card, or launcher for this folder |
| Idea-formulation strategies | Hunt for expensive, frequent problems — still need spread test before capital | “I generated an idea, therefore borrow” |

If nothing in the right column is true and the spread test fails, **do not borrow**. The pension path is slow; a failed loop is fast.
