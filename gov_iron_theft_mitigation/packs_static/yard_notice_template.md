# Notice to scrap / metal dealers — public infrastructure metal

**Issued by:** _(Local Government / licensing office)_  
**Date:** _(insert)_  

## Message

Do not buy or accept ferrous metal that appears to be public infrastructure, including manhole covers, roadside railings, barriers, and similarly marked council or MDA assets.

Dealers must:

1. Refuse unmarked lots that look like covers, gratings, or barrier sections  
2. Refuse any piece showing a government / council public mark or ID  
3. Keep a purchase record (seller name, phone, date, weight, description) as required by local rules  
4. Cooperate with lawful inspection by licensing officers and police  

Stolen public metal endangers residents through open chambers and missing barriers. Buying it may lead to licence action and criminal investigation.
