# Public Iron Asset Protect: Video Production Package

**Source:** Claude (owner import, was `Nu.md`)  
**Companion visual:** `chatgpt_workflow_storyboard.jpg` (ChatGPT 6-panel board)  
**Ops truth:** `../SOLUTION_PLAYBOOK.md`  

Civic-tech explainer on protecting public iron assets (manhole covers, railings, roadside barricades) in a Nigerian city. Runtime about 2:15, with a 5-second end card.

**Honesty note:** Any Day-90 KPI figures or named LGA labels in companion mockups are demo fiction until a real pilot runs.
Global specs
• Format: 16:9, 3840×2160, 24 fps, no watermark, no real brands.
• Privacy: license plates blurred; phone numbers shown only as "+234 ••• ••• ••••".
• Palette: forest green #0F4D3A, teal #1FA38F, warm daylight tones, deep slate #0E1B22 for ops-desk scenes.
• Typography: Inter or Source Sans, bold titles.
• Camera: slow push-ins and gentle pans on a gimbal or slider, soft depth of field, no handheld shake.
• Structure: each stage opens with a bold title card, then a lower-third subtitle.
• Tone: professional, hopeful, competent, serious. No horror, no crime-thriller glamor, no instructions for thieves, no lock-picking, no bypassing sensors or yard checks.
Storyboard
Cold open (0:00, 8s)
Dawn over a Nigerian city street, with traffic, market stalls, and a clean manhole cover in the foreground.
On-screen text: "Public iron is public safety."
1. PREPARE (0:08–0:30)
Subtitle: Map. Mark. Roster.
• Drone push-in on a map of one LGA, with teal pins appearing on corridors.
• Workers in hi-vis photograph a cover and stamp a small ID mark. Overlay: LGA-017 / MH-0342.
• A supervisor on a tablet builds an on-call roster (names shown as "Dispatcher A", "Crew 2").
2. PREVENT (0:30–0:52)
Subtitle: Frame it. Log it. Make it low-value.
• Close-up of a retained frame and marked cover.
• A contractor logs removed metal on a clipboard or tablet (weight, source, destination).
• A replacement cover goes in, a standards-compliant lower-resale type. Overlay: "Meets design standard." Show no tampering detail.
3. DETECT (0:52–1:15)
Subtitle: Two signals, one ticket.
• An icon of a cover pulses teal when a tilt/lift sensor triggers (graphic only).
• A citizen's phone shows a generic chat report with a map pin and a photo.
• Both streams animate into a ticket card on a night ops dashboard.
4. RESPOND (1:15–1:42)
Subtitle: Acknowledge. Secure. Close.
• A dispatcher clicks "Acknowledge", with a soft radio click.
• The crew arrives in daylight, sets a temporary barricade, then installs a replacement cover.
• A status bar advances: New → Acknowledged → Assigned → Secured → Closed.
• Two badges with live clocks, Ack < 30 min and Secure < 4 hrs, both turning green.
5. DENY CASH-OUT (1:42–2:02)
Subtitle: No market for public iron.
• A scrap-yard gate with a sign: "We do not accept marked or government-marked public iron."
• An inspector with a checklist (register, weighbridge log, marked-item check).
• A weekly hotspot heatmap goes to a "Police Liaison" icon. This is enforcement coordination only, with no evasion content.
6. LEARN / SCALE (2:02–2:15)
Subtitle: Measure. Expand.
• A Day-90 scorecard with green KPIs: median acknowledgement time, secure time, repeat-theft rate, open tickets.
• The map zooms out and the corridor highlight extends to the next LGA.
End card (5s)
Forest-green background, centered text:
Public Iron Asset Protect
Detect fast. Secure fast. Hard to cash. Measure. Scale.
Audio
• Music: restrained, warm contemporary African instrumental with soft kora or guitar plucks, light percussion, and a gentle pad. No lyrics, building slightly toward the scorecard.
• Sound design: city ambience (distant traffic, birds, generators at low level); a soft two-note alert chime on ticket creation; a radio squelch click on dispatch; a short "confirm" tone as each status turns green. No sirens.
Sample AI video prompt (per shot)
Photoreal cinematic 4K, 16:9, slow gimbal push-in. Two municipal workers in hi-vis and hard hats photograph and stamp an ID mark on a clean manhole cover on a busy Nigerian city street in warm morning light. Soft depth of field, stable, documentary tone, no text, no logos.
Keep overlays out of the generated shots and add them in editing (After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, or Canva) so the typography stays sharp.
