## 1. Executive summary

Theft of public iron (manhole covers, railings, roadside barricades, and similar assets) leaves open holes, flood risk, and traffic danger. Citizens often notice the loss days later. Stolen metal is still easy to sell as scrap.

We propose a **90-day pilot in one Local Government Area** that helps your Works and ICT teams **detect loss faster**, **prove ownership of assets**, and **dispatch crews to secure or replace** high-risk covers. Policing, scrap-yard licensing, and prosecution remain with the appropriate authorities. We supply the detection and operations system that supports them.

**Ask:** Approve a fixed-scope pilot MoU for one LGA corridor set (20–50 life-safety assets), with clear KPIs and a go / no-go review at day 90.

---

## 2. The problem for this LGA

| Impact | What residents and the Council face |
|--------|-------------------------------------|
| Safety | Open chambers and missing barriers cause injury and accidents |
| Cost | Emergency replacements and flood damage after covers disappear |
| Trust | Repeated “hole in the road” complaints with slow close-out |
| Leakage | Public metal leaves the corridor and enters the scrap chain |

Cameras alone do not solve this if nobody is alerted in time and if stolen iron can still be cashed at yards. The pilot attacks **time-to-know** and **time-to-secure**, and supports yard engagement that your licensing and police partners lead.

---

## 3. What we propose (pilot scope)

### In scope (90 days)

1. **Asset register** for the pilot set: photo, unique ID mark, location, asset type, last check date  
2. **Public report channel** (WhatsApp and/or USSD): “missing cover / barrier here” with location pin into a ticket list  
3. **Sensor package** on **20–50** life-safety covers or equivalent high-risk points (sites chosen with Works)  
4. **Ops dashboard** for Works / ICT: alert or report → assign crew → mark secured or replaced  
5. **Named on-call path** so night alerts are not orphaned (Council names responders, or we provide first-line triage under the MoU)  
6. **Weekly hotspot pack** for Works and Area Command liaison (maps and counts only, no operational secrets in public)  
7. **Yard engagement pack** for Council use: plain-language notice and inspection checklist (Council / police enforce)  
8. **Day-90 report** with KPIs and scale recommendation  

### Out of scope for this pilot

- Replacing the Nigeria Police Force or scrap licensing authority  
- City-wide sensor coverage on every cover  
- Any work that would publish how to defeat sensors, locks, or yard checks

## 5. How it works (plain language)

```
Citizen report ──┐
                 ├──► Ops desk / dashboard ──► Works crew (secure / replace)
Sensor alert  ───┘              │
                                ├──► Weekly hotspot pack ──► Police liaison
                                └──► Asset ID on metal ──► supports yard checks
```

1. A cover is lifted or a citizen reports a missing asset.  
2. Works sees a ticket within minutes (pilot sites) or within the report channel SLA.  
3. A crew secures the hole or fits a replacement under your existing works rules.  
4. Weekly packs show where losses cluster so patrols and yard checks can focus.

## 7. Success measures (KPIs)

Agree targets at kick-off. Suggested defaults for discussion:

| KPI | Suggested pilot target |
|-----|------------------------|
| Time from sensor alert to Works acknowledgement | Under 30 minutes during on-call hours |
| Time from confirmed missing life-safety cover to secure / barricade | Under 4 hours (or your Works SLA if stricter) |
| Share of public reports that become closed tickets | At least 90% logged with outcome |
| Orphan alerts (alert with no acknowledgement) | Under 5% of sensor alerts |
| Day-90 briefing delivered to Chairman / Works / ICT | Yes / no |

Final numbers are set in the MoU annex so both sides share one scorecard.
