## 1. Legal and money floor (all lines)

Before any paid invoice on **any** line (A–D):

| # | Requirement | Done when |
|---|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Nigerian limited company (CAC) + tax number | Registration docs on file |
| 2 | Professional indemnity that matches the line you are selling | Policy wording names readiness / authorised testing / ongoing watch / risk advice as relevant; premium **check this** |
| 3 | Written contract templates that state what you are **not** | Not the certifier; not a general “licence to hack”; not physical SecaaS; not a guarantee the regulator will be happy |
| 4 | Personal-data rule for anything you see as advisor or processor | NDPA-aware; written processing terms when you handle client personal data (**check this** with counsel) |
| 5 | One public page (or equivalent sheet) listing offer lines and refusals | Buyer can see A–D and the “we do not…” list before they pay |

You do **not** need on day one: night rota, QSA listing, DPCO licence, or all-week watch desk. Those appear when a specific line requires them (see `offer_lines.md`).

CAC registration and bank-account opening stay **owner actions** until a later lock flips them into this pack as “done.”

## 2. Claims you never print

| Never say | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| “We certified you” / “NDPR certified” | Stamp is not yours; NDPR is not the live instrument after GAID |
| “We are licensed to hack” | Permission is the **written letter** for named systems and dates (Cybercrimes Act s.6) |
| “24/7 physical + cyber covered” | Physical is not a sold product; 24/7 IT only when Line C rota + power exist |
| Attack steps, tool recipes, “how we got in” in brochures | Line B ends before method |
| “We issue ISO / PCI / Commission certificates” | Unless a later lock flips and you hold that separate status |

Put “we do not issue this certificate” in proposal, contract, and invoice for Line A.

## 3. Shared delivery rules

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| IT only as a sold security product | Advise on physical DC controls in writing if useful; do **not** sell guard / CCTV-ops / gate staffing |
| Separate contracts per shape | Cert project ≠ test letter ≠ monthly watch ≠ risk engagement |
| Open order | Prefer **A** before heavy **C**; do not fold **B** into **C**’s monthly fee; do not open **C** before the client’s named system list exists |
| Lean firm | Essential skills documented in `lean_roles.md`; outsource / fractional OK; one person ≠ all lines at once |
| Research vs company | Teaching / DC notes stay in `dc-security-nigeria-research/`; this folder is the company requirements home |

## 5. What “ready for first paid work” means

Upstream Drive is ready for a **first** paid engagement when:

1. The floor in section 1 is honest (or gaps are explicit owner blocks, not silent).  
2. The chosen line’s refusals and deliverables match `offer_lines.md`.  
3. No brochure claim from section 2 appears in the proposal.  
4. The person who will deliver that line is identified (lean roster).  

Not required for first invoice: Project_reminder auto-open (Phase 5), brand pitch (Phase 4). Line **D** requires `risk_management_requirements.md` (now present) and an owner read before first D invoice.
