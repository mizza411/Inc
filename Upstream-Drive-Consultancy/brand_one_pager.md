# Brand one-pager - pitch skeleton

**Company:** Upstream Drive Consultancy
**Task:** UDC-1, Phase 4
**Date:** 22 Nov 2026
**Who it is for:** A short sheet a buyer can read before they pay.
**Not this sheet:** A contract, a website, a CAC filing, or a price list.

## One line

Upstream Drive Consultancy is a Nigeria-first firm. It helps a named client prepare for security and privacy reviews, test only what a written letter allows, watch a named set of IT alerts, and give risk advice. It does not issue certificates, and it does not sell physical guard services.

## What the firm sells

| Line | Plain offer |
|------|-------------|
| A | A gap list, policies, evidence coaching, and training for ISO/IEC 27001, Nigeria Data Protection Act, or PCI DSS readiness. The stamp stays with the body that issues it. |
| B | A scoped report on named systems and named dates, and only after a written authorisation letter. The default is observe-only. |
| C | Acknowledge alerts on a named list, inside the hours that were sold, and tell the client's named person. |
| D | A risk picture, priorities, and treatment options for a named owner. The client keeps the residual risk. |

The full lines stay in `offer_lines.md` and `risk_management_requirements.md`.

## What the firm refuses

- It does not say it certified the client.
- It does not say it is licensed to hack. Permission is the written letter.
- It does not sell physical patrol, guards, or CCTV operations.
- It does not print 24/7 for a one-person weekday watch.
- It does not put attack steps in a brochure.

The full refusal list stays in `company_requirements.md`.

## Before the first invoice

Registration, tax, professional indemnity that names the line being sold, a contract that states the refusals, and a personal-data rule are the floor. Those stay owner actions until the owner says they are done. The checklist stays in `company_requirements.md`.

## Lean firm

One person does not cover lines A to D at once. Seats are core, fractional, or outsourced. The roster stays in `lean_roles.md`.

## How to use this skeleton

Copy a line into a later proposal only after the floor in `company_requirements.md` is true for that line. This file does not open on a schedule. The daily open stays `offer_lines.docx` only.
