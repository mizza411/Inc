## 1. Workshop review checklist

**Service name:** Workshop Revenue and Parts-Control Review  
**Sample size (pilot):** up to 25 completed job records, selected parts and payment records, limited discount/refund sample  
**One location.** No full physical stocktake in the ₦30,000 offer.

### A. Job records and customer payments
- [ ] Completed jobs match invoices and payment records
- [ ] Extra work has documented customer approval
- [ ] Recorded payments match bank, POS, or cash records the owner authorises
- [ ] Missing or incomplete invoices are listed as control gaps

### B. Spare parts and inventory
- [ ] Sample parts purchases match purchase records
- [ ] Parts issued to job cards match parts charged to customers
- [ ] Returns, unused parts, and warranty replacements are reviewed
- [ ] Small stock sample vs inventory register (only if in scope and paid separately for a fuller visit)

### C. Discounts, refunds, and adjustments
- [ ] Discounts follow the owner's authorised rules
- [ ] Voids, refunds, and price adjustments have supporting documentation
- [ ] Repeated exceptions are flagged for explanation
- [ ] Corrections show an approval trail

### D. Management controls
- [ ] Who can approve purchases, discounts, refunds, write-offs is clear
- [ ] One person does not control every stage of a transaction (note if they do)
- [ ] Daily or weekly reconciliation steps are agreed
- [ ] Owner or supervisor knows how to review exceptions fairly

**Rule:** Missing records = control weakness to document. Not automatic proof of dishonesty.

## 3. Sample monthly report structure

Use this shape for the pilot debrief and later retainers. **Fictional example only.**

**Title:** Workshop Integrity and Loss-Prevention Report  
**Client:** Example Auto Workshop  
**Period:** October 2026  
**Marking:** Confidential

### Summary
- Records reviewed: 25  
- Exceptions flagged: 4  

| Reference | Observation | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| J-014 | Payment record not matched | Reconcile with bank or POS records |
| J-019 | Parts issued exceeded parts billed | Check warranty and job notes |
| J-021 | Discount approval missing | Obtain approval evidence |
| J-024 | Invoice reference missing | Confirm transaction documentation |

**Plain line for every report:** An exception is not a finding of theft or employee misconduct.

### Management action plan
1. Reconcile each exception and document the explanation.
2. Require recorded approval for discounts and parts write-offs.
3. Introduce a daily job-card and payment reconciliation.
4. Review open actions at the next monthly meeting.

### Executive conclusion (template)
“N exceptions need follow-up. Causes and any financial impact stay unconfirmed until supporting records are reviewed.”

## 4. 30-day action checklist

### Week 1 — Validate
- [ ] Keep auto workshops as the first niche to test
- [ ] Interview 10 owners
- [ ] Write down the five most common control problems they name
- [ ] Confirm at least three will discuss a paid pilot
- [ ] If workshops have almost no records, pivot test to a distributor or retailer with digital records

### Week 2 — Prepare
- [ ] Print or copy this checklist for field use
- [ ] Build a simple reconciliation spreadsheet (job vs invoice vs payment vs parts)
- [ ] Draft a narrow service agreement and privacy procedure
- [ ] Lock pilot price ₦30,000 and written exclusions
- [ ] Get a Nigerian professional to review contract scope before paid work (budget reserve)

### Week 3 — Sell
- [ ] List 30 prospects (about 10 workshops, 10 spare-parts, 10 distributors or building materials)
- [ ] Abuja lead areas to walk or call: Apo Mechanics Village, Gudu spare-parts, Dei-Dei building materials (leads only, not endorsements)
- [ ] Contact at least 20 owners
- [ ] Ask the pay question: would you pay for an independent review with a practical plan?
- [ ] Secure one paid pilot and written authorisation for the records you will see

### Week 4 — Deliver
- [ ] Complete the review within the agreed sample
- [ ] Verify exceptions with the owner before final wording
- [ ] Present report and prioritised actions
- [ ] Log your real hours and expenses
- [ ] Offer monthly monitoring only after the pilot lands
- [ ] Decide: repeat the offer, change price or scope, or change niche

### Success measures (not “thieves caught”)
- Two paid pilots, or clear evidence of why buyers said no
- One repeatable method
- One report workflow you can reuse
- At least one client open to monthly monitoring
- Real delivery cost vs price you can defend
