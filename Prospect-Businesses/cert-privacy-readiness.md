## Problem

A Nigerian firm with no internal security team is being pushed by a bank, a foreign customer, a tender, or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission to show ISO 27001 readiness, Nigeria Data Protection Act readiness, or PCI readiness. They need a gap list, policies, and staff who understand the ask. They need the name of whoever actually issues the stamp.

## Why this is a prospect

- Mandatory and expensive when a bank, a tender, or the Commission is already asking. Training repeats. It is urgent only while that ask is open.
- Fits Nigeria now because those asks land on firms that still have no security team. The research pack already maps this as Offer A.
- Fits work already in this repo (Upstream Drive offer lines and the IT security research). This is a service.

## MVP sketch

- **Scope:** One written gap list against one named target (ISO 27001 readiness, Nigeria Data Protection Act readiness, or PCI readiness). Policies and an evidence list a reviewer will ask for. A workshop with an agenda and a sign-in sheet. A short risk picture for the named decision-maker (priorities and treatment choices). A restore drill only when that buyer asks whether an older copy can actually be opened. The handoff names who stamps. This card does not include authorised testing or a monthly watch desk.
- **Est. cost (USD):** No software build budget. Company, contract, and insurance come before the first invoice. Figures are not set in this file.
- **Platforms:** Written pack and a live workshop. No new web app, tray, or phone app.
