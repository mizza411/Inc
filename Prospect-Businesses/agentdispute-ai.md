## Problem

OPay and peers champion AI-powered digital finance (Techpoint, 2026-07-10), but agent-banking networks still burn hours on disputed transfers, failed withdrawals, and float variance. Super-agents need a B2B dispute desk, not another consumer wallet.

## Why this is a prospect

- **GUEMF:** Growing / Urgent / Expensive / Frequent (composite **21**; Mandatory moderate)
- Daily ops pain with per-agent or per-ticket pricing potential
- Strong founder fit: fintech APIs, workflow SaaS, optional LLM assist

## MVP sketch

- **Scope:** Ingest agent tickets → classify dispute type → evidence checklist → SLA timer → CSV export for super-agent / PSP ops (AI assist, human decision)  
- **Est. cost (USD):** $14k–$28k  
- **Platforms:** web (ops desk) + optional WhatsApp alerts  
- **Target:** Super-agents, fintech ops teams, POS aggregators  
- **Regulatory notes:** CBN agent banking / PSP context; NDPA; do not store full PAN; partner data-sharing agreements
