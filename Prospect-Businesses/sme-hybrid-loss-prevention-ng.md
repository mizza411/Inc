## Problem

Owner-managed SMEs in Nigeria (starting with auto workshops) lose money through weak job, parts, payment, and approval controls. Owners cannot watch every transaction, and ordinary mystery shopping framed as “catch thieves” is risky and hard to sell. They need discreet process reviews, practical fixes, and optional recurring checks.

## Why this is a prospect

- **Expensive / frequent / growing:** small till and parts leaks compound, and multi-employee cash or inventory businesses feel it often
- Fits Nigeria now: many SMEs have basic records but no internal audit team, especially in Abuja workshop and parts clusters
- Fits a lean operator: service-first (checklist + spreadsheet + report), selective on-site checks, coaching, automation only after paid repeats
- Owner already chose prevention and coaching over entrapment, hybrid model (Option C), under ₦250k launch, workshop niche to test first
- **No app required** for the first milestone (one paying client, one useful report, one repeatable process)

## MVP sketch

- **Scope:** Workshop Revenue and Parts-Control Review — sample up to ~25 completed jobs, selected parts and payment checks, concise findings report, 30-minute owner debrief. Later: controls coaching and monthly monitoring. Not a SaaS portal at start.
- **Est. cost (USD):** well under ~$30k — plan under ₦250,000 launch (~₦200k allocated + ₦50k buffer). Founding pilot offer hypothesis ~₦30,000
- **Platforms:** service delivery (spreadsheet + report). Optional light scripts later. No mobile app / client portal in v0
- **Target:** independent auto workshops first (Abuja), then spare-parts or small distributors if workshops lack records or will not pay
- **Regulatory notes:** NDPA-aware handling of client records, written scope, exception ≠ proven theft, Nigerian legal review before aggressive or regulated investigative work
