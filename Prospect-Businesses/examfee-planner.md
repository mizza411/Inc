- **Est. cost (USD):** $8k–$18k  
- **Platforms:** web + WhatsApp  
- **Target:** Private secondary schools, exam centres, parent associations, edtech payment aggregators  
- **Regulatory notes:** Not exam-body official; NDPA for student/parent PII; partner licensed PSPs for collections
