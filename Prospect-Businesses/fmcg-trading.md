## Problem

IT contract income arrives in lumps and then stops. Everyday goods that people use up and buy again can bring in smaller payments more often. This file records a trading prospect for that. It does not start the shop.

## Why this is a prospect

- Frequent, because soap and washing powder were a 30-day purchase for most households in the NBS 2023/24 survey
- Fits Nigeria now because small packs already sell through kiosks and open markets
- The first line is a known laundry-detergent pack, which sits outside the food-price shock and outside the weak personal-care quarter
- The 14-day test note already exists, and this card does not start that test

## MVP sketch

- **Scope:** Buy small packs and resell them for same-day money. The lead pack is laundry detergent that kiosks already trust. Seasoning, cooking oil, and noodles stay on the test card. No factory, no new brand, and no app.

- **Platforms:** trading by hand. Households within walking distance, plus 5 to 10 kiosks.
