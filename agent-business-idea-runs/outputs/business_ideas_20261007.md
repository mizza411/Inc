### 1. SoftTest RiskTier Prep Desk — NITDA mandatory testing risk-tier readiness (**S12 Mode A**)

- **Problem:** BusinessDay (7 Oct 2026) and NGSTQB/TechBuild: NITDA’s April 2026 **National Software Testing Guideline** requires software deployed in Nigeria to be tested/certified by a **NITDA-licensed** organisation before operational use. Industry backs the rules but pushes **risk-based / proportionate** enforcement so low-harm tools are not treated like critical systems. Vendors, integrators, and MDA suppliers lack a single risk-tier evidence pack (data sensitivity, public exposure, failure consequence) before paid LSTO engagement.
- **Solution:** Risk-tier questionnaire → artifact checklist (security/performance/usability notes) → LSTO handoff PDF → change-trigger log when significant mods may need re-cert (**not** a licensed testing lab itself).
- **Target:** Nigerian SaaS/vendors selling to MDAs or regulated sectors; digital agencies shipping client systems.
- **MVP cost:** $8k–$18k.
- **Regulatory:** Informational only — not NITDA, NGSTQB, or an LSTO; NDPA for any test artifacts containing personal data.
- **Competitors / alternatives:**
  - **NITDA guideline PDFs / portal notices** — official; no prep UX. Why Nigeria buyer still chooses this: risk-tier checklist before spend.
  - **Big-4 / QA consultancies** — expensive. Why still us: Naira micro-desk for SMEs.
  - **DIY spreadsheet of test cases** — common; misses re-cert triggers. Why still us: LSTO handoff PDF.
  - **SMECyber Week Shield (prior)** — generic cyber hygiene. Why still us: **mandatory SoftTest / risk-tier** job.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A **5/5/4/5/4 = 23** (Growing SoftTest market; Urgent as enforcement debate heats; Expensive failed deploys / rework; Mandatory under Guideline; Frequent release/mod cycles).
