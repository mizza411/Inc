- **Problem:** Under NTA 2025 §202 / §56, firms providing professional services are categorically excluded from “small company” 0% CIT even when turnover ≤ ₦50m and assets ≤ ₦250m — many boutique consultancies, clinics, and law chambers still budget as if exemption applies.
- **Solution:** Classification checklist (service codes vs retail/SME peers) → projected CIT/Dev Levy delta → accountant handoff PDF + filing calendar (**not** ZeroCite “still file at 0%” for exempt SMEs).
- **Target:** Incorporated professional practices (law, accounting, consulting, clinics, architecture/engineering boutiques) and their bookkeepers.
- **MVP cost:** $10k–$22k.
- **Regulatory:** Educational / informational only — not licensed tax advice; NDPA for firm profile fields.
- **Competitors / alternatives:**
  - **Big-4 / mid-tier tax firms** — thorough; expensive for micros. Why Nigeria buyer still chooses this: Naira-priced self-serve reclass checklist.
  - **TaxPro Max / Rev360 DIY** — filing rails; weak classification coaching. Why still us: “am I excluded?” decision tree.
  - **ZeroCite Records (prior)** — zero-CIT filing hygiene. Why still us: professional-services **exclusion** path.
  - **LinkedIn/legal blog posts** — awareness only. Why still us: productized calendar + handoff PDF.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A 5/5/5/5/4 = **24** (Growing 2026 NTA rollout; Urgent cash planning; Expensive 30% vs 0%; Mandatory statutory class; Frequent annual + quarterly prep).
