### 1. ShareCap Returns Desk — Issuing/acquiring market-structure readiness (**S12 Mode A**)

- **Problem:** CBN’s 15 Jun 2026 payments circular imposes cross-segment market-share limits (e.g. >25% consumer issuing → ≤15% merchant acquiring in the same rolling 12 months, and the reverse) with **full compliance by 31 Dec 2026** and **monthly market-share returns**. Group-level “related entities” counting makes spreadsheet guessing dangerous for banks/MMOs/PSPs near thresholds.
- **Solution:** Segment map (issuing vs acquiring) → related-entity inventory → rolling-share estimate board → monthly return checklist → board memo for remediation options (**not** data-centre migration; **not** e-invoice IRN onboarding).
- **Target:** Compliance/product leads at DMBs, MMOs, PTSPs/PSSPs near concentration thresholds; boutique payments counsel white-labelling the checklist.
- **MVP cost:** $12k–$26k.
- **Regulatory:** Educational readiness desk; not a CBN licence application; figures attributed to public circular summaries.
- **Competitors / alternatives:**
  - **Big-4 / top-tier law firms** — deep advice; FX-heavy. Why Nigeria buyer still chooses this: Naira-priced monthly-return ops board.
  - **Internal BI / Excel** — common; weak group-entity rollup. Why still us: related-entity + dual-segment template.
  - **LocalData Rail Prep (Jul 27)** — Jan 2027 data residency. Why still us: Dec 2026 share-cap + returns job.
  - **DIY reading of circular PDFs** — free; no countdown board. Why still us: recurring monthly return workflow.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A **5/5/5/5/4 = 24** (Growing concentration oversight; Urgent ahead of Dec 2026; Expensive forced divest/restructure; Mandatory for affected licensees; Frequent monthly returns). Evidence: BusinessDay market-share limits — https://businessday.ng/news/article/cbn-curbs-payments-dominance-with-new-market-share-limits/ ; Olaniwun Ajayi summary — https://www.olaniwunajayi.net/blog/decoding-the-cbns-new-market-structure-and-data-localisation-directives/ ; Digital Policy Alert timeline — https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/19869-market-structure-requirements-in-circular-pssdirpubcir001004-on-the-nigeria-payments-system
