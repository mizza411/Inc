### 1. BuyerVAT Deny Desk — Buyer VAT-input denial / trading-partner IRN war room (**S12 Mode A**)

- **Problem:** After the 31 Jul 2026 large-taxpayer e-invoicing window closed, NRS enforcement makes unvalidated invoices ineligible for **VAT input credit** and often for tax-deductible expense treatment — so **buyers** take cash hits when suppliers still issue non-MBS invoices (₦200k + 100% surcharge + interest hit issuers too). Distinct from issuer 24h reject queues and mid-band readiness boards.
- **Solution:** Supplier IRN completeness sample → AP hold rules for missing IRN/QR → VAT-input exposure worksheet → supplier chase pack → board memo for procurement (**≠** RejectWindow issuer 24h; **≠** FiscalLive penalty inventory; **≠** MidBand Live; **≠** IRN Accept Gate alone).
- **Target:** AP/tax leads at large taxpayers and multi-entity buying groups; boutique tax implementers white-labelling the pack.
- **MVP cost:** $10k–$22k.
- **Regulatory:** Educational buyer process desk; not licensed tax advice; not an NRS APP/SI; NDPA for vendor lists.
- **Competitors / alternatives:**
  - **APP/SI vendor portals** — rail-focused; weak buyer VAT-exposure board. Why Nigeria buyer still chooses this: input-credit denial framing across suppliers.
  - **ERP AP modules** — generic holds. Why still us: NRS IRN/VAT denial playbook.
  - **IRN Accept Gate / RejectWindow / FiscalLive / MidBand Live (Jul 25–Aug 1)** — adjacent jobs. Why still us: post-enforcement **buyer credit** war room.
  - **DIY Excel of missing IRNs** — common; no exposure math. Why still us: VAT denial + chase timer.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A **5/5/5/5/5 = 25** (Growing enforcement; Urgent live sanctions; Expensive denied VAT; Mandatory for large cohort; Frequent invoice batches). Evidence: BusinessDay enforcement — https://businessday.ng/news/article/nrs-begins-enforcement-as-e-invoicing-compliance-deadline-lapses/ ; Vanguard penalties — https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/nrs-e-invoicing-deadline-passes-as-large-taxpayers-face-penalties/ ; Guardian buyer-credit framing — https://guardian.ng/opinion/the-final-24-hours-is-your-business-ready-for-e-invoicing/ ; OrchidaTax input-credit denial note — https://orchidatax.com/country-compliance/nigeria-regulations/
- **Commercial viability:** High — cash VAT risk is live after Jul-31.
- **Dedup:** High vs RejectWindow / FiscalLive / IRN Accept / MidBand Live / SupplierMBS / EmergeInvoice.
- **Founder fit:** Strong.
