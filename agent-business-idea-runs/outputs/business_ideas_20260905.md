- **Solution:** Guided filing calendar → screenshot/HAR/upload-failure logger → timestamped “attempt evidence” PDF for accountant/NRS correspondence → reminder before next statutory cut-off (**not** tax computation; **not** ZeroCite zero-liability records; **not** TaxAuthority Route Desk).

  - **LCCI / chamber circulars** — advocacy; not an ops desk. Why still us: firm-level attempt log.
  - **Accountant WhatsApp** — human; inconsistent evidence. Why still us: structured timestamps.
  - **DIY screenshot folder** — common; weak for penalty disputes. Why still us: exportable PDF trail.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A 5/5/5/5/5 = **25** (Growing post-Tax Admin Act; Urgent filing/penalty windows; Expensive penalties + advisor time; Mandatory CIT filing; Frequent annual + VAT cycles).
