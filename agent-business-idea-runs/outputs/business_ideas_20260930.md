- **GUEMF:** Mode A **5/5/5/5/4 = 24** (Growing reform committee + 2026 regs; Urgent before enforcement; Expensive diversion/mispricing risk; Mandatory customs/tax on domestic entry; Frequent invoice/exit cycles).
