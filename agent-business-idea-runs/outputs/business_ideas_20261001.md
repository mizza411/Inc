### 1. InterestOrder DualBook Desk — Oct 1 late-tax dual accrual + monthly rate watch (**S12 Mode A**)

- **Problem:** From **1 October 2026**, interest on late naira tax is MPR+1% (floor: 364-day T-bill yield), FX liabilities use SOFR+6, interest is calculated **daily**, one rate applies per calendar month, and NRS must **publish** the applicable rate by the **3rd business day** each month. Interest that arose before Oct 1 stays on old rules, interest arising from Oct 1 (including on older principal) uses the new order — dual books confuse Mid-SMEs and bookkeepers. Distinct from **LateTax MPR+1 Desk** (2026-09-29), which estimated the formula before the effective date.
- **Solution:** Dual accrual worksheet (pre-Oct 1 vs from-Oct 1) → monthly NRS publish watch calendar → SOFR/FX liability flag → 10% late-payment penalty reminder → accountant handoff PDF (**not** tax filing software, **not** a waiver petition bot).
- **Target:** Mid-SME finance managers, bookkeepers, and multi-state taxpayers facing NRS + SIRS.
- **MVP cost:** $8k–$18k.
- **Regulatory:** Informational only — not NRS/SIRS/FCT tax authority advice; not a substitute for licensed tax practice; NDPA for taxpayer profile fields.
- **Competitors / alternatives:**
  - **NRS website rate table DIY** — official when published; no dual-book UX. Why Nigeria buyer still chooses this: pre/post-Oct 1 split + publish watch.
  - **Accountant WhatsApp** — human; slow at month turn. Why still us: structured dual worksheet.
  - **LateTax MPR+1 Desk (2026-09-29)** — pre-effective estimator. Why still us: **effective-day dual book + monthly publish cadence**.
  - **Excel Interest Calc** — common; misses FX SOFR track. Why still us: SOFR+6 flag + penalty note.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A **5/5/5/5/5 = 25** (Growing market-linked interest; Urgent effective today; Expensive daily interest + 10% penalty; Mandatory across NRS/SIRS/FCT; Frequent monthly rate + daily accrual).
