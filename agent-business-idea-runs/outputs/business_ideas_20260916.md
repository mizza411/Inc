### 1. CAC Batch6 Rescue Desk — Strike-off list → AR + PSC proof pack (**S12 Mode A**)

- **Problem:** CAC Batch 6 lists ~**100,000** companies for possible strike-off over outstanding annual returns and other statutory filings (incl. PSC/beneficial ownership). - **Solution:** List-check helper → outstanding AR + PSC checklist → filing order → proof ZIP + email cover to CAC address → calendar to deadline (**not** a generic “file annual returns by 30 Sep” reminder for all limited companies).
- **Target:** Owners/secretaries of Batch 6–listed SMEs; boutique company-secretarial shops packaging the rescue.
- **MVP cost:** $8k–$18k.
- **Regulatory:** Educational alignment to CAC public notice / CAMA strike-off path; not a substitute for a licensed company secretary; NDPA for PSC identity fields.
- **Competitors / alternatives:**

  - **Company secretary retainer** — correct but scarce/expensive for micros. Why still us: Naira-priced one-shot rescue.
  - **CAC30Sept Return Desk (prior)** — routine 30 Sep AR filing. Why still us: Batch 6 strike-off list + PSC + proof email job.
  - **DIY WhatsApp accountant** — common; misses proof-to-CAC step. Why still us: structured packet.
- **GUEMF:** Mode A 5/5/5/5/4 = **24** (Growing cleanup batches; Urgent ~Oct window; Expensive if struck off / banking KYC fails; Mandatory CAMA filings; Frequent batch exercises).
