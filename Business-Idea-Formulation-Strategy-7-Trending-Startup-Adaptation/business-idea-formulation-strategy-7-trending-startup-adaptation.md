# Strategy 7: Trending Startup Adaptation

## 🎯 Core Strategy
Generate business ideas by adapting trending products/startups for implementation in Nigeria, differentiated by combining with different niches or concepts.

## Process

### Step 1: Get trending products (primary — Product Hunt)
1. Open [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) or RSS: `https://www.producthunt.com/feed`
2. List 8–15 recent launches (name + short description)
3. Optionally cross-check [Techpoint Africa](https://techpoint.africa/) for Nigeria-relevant launches

### Step 1b (optional legacy): Crunchbase Trending Profiles
1. Go to [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/)
2. Use Ctrl + F to search for "Trending Profiles"
3. Take a screenshot of the "Trending Profiles" section

### Prompt 1a
**Give me ideas based on this to implement in Nigeria. Your ideas should be differentiated from the ideas present in the source by combining with a different niche or concept (these different niches do not compulsorily have to be present in the source) in order to generate new startup ideas).**

**[Paste Product Hunt list — or legacy: paste screenshot of Trending Profiles]**

This method identifies what's currently trending globally and adapts it for the Nigerian market, ensuring you're working with proven, in-demand concepts.
