# Strategy 9: Financial News Problem Extraction

## 🎯 Core Strategy
Extract problems that can be solved with digital solutions by analyzing content from Nigerian financial and business news websites on a scheduled basis.

## Process

### Scheduled Content Collection
**IMPORTANT**: Use "CTRL + A" to select ENTIRE WEBSITE CONTENT AND FEED into ChatGPT on the specified days:

- **Mondays & Tuesdays**: [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/)
- **Wednesdays & Thursdays**: [Financial Nigeria](https://www.financialnigeria.com/)
- **Fridays & Saturdays**: [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/)
