## 2. What “air-gap” means here (plain words)

**Air-gap** (working definition for this note): systems or networks that are **intentionally isolated** from the open internet or from less-trusted networks, so updates, data, and admin work follow **controlled paths** (removable media, data diodes, jump hosts, offline build rooms, and similar).

Buyers care about air-gap when:

- A regulator or board expects **isolation** for critical systems
- A breach on the office LAN must **not** reach OT / SCADA / CNII-adjacent gear
- Cloud tools are **off the table** for a named environment
- Vendors keep pushing “always online” products that do not fit that environment

Paul Pearl’s job in this slice is **framing and advice**, not inventing a new product brand beside the locked offer lines.

| SMEs growing into tenders | First isolation story for one critical box or room | A then D; do not open C before a named system list |

### 3.2 Phrases that signal demand (research cues)

Use these as **search and conversation cues**, not as auto-submitted outreach:

- “I need a” … offline / isolated / air-gapped / segregated environment  
- “I don’t like it when” … vendors force cloud-only admin / always-on agents  
- “We cannot connect that VLAN to the internet”  
- “Updates only by approved media / diode”  
- “Evidence room must stay offline”

MacroDroid / phone phrase lists can feed a later skim; this note does **not** store personal automation data.
