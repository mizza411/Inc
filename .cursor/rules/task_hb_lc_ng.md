## Owner ask (2026-08-17)

Research high-barrier, low-competition Nigerian market opportunities that AI is not currently doing.

Chat scan 2026-08-17 is discovery only. It is not a product, a prospect card, or permission to buy kit.

## Filter (locked unless the owner changes it in Phase 0)

1. **High barrier** = licence, capital kit, certified people, physical site, or legal liability.
2. **Low competition** = few *formal* operators versus demand, or legally mandated capacity still missing.
3. **AI-resistant** = a chatbot or code agent cannot hold the licence, move the goods, stamp a lab result, or take statutory liability.

Drop POS, mini-import, generic SaaS, AI wrappers, content farms, ExamFee, SCIMLite, AgentDispute, SME Compliance Lite checklists, Tegrid IMS, FIR *report* product, and the agric farm-input pilot.
