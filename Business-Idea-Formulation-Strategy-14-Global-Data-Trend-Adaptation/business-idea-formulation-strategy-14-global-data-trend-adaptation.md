# Strategy 14: Global Data Trend Adaptation

## 🎯 Core Strategy
Generate business ideas by adapting global data trends from OurWorldInData for implementation in Nigeria, differentiated by combining with different niches or concepts.

## Process

### Step 1: Get OurWorldInData Content
1. Go to [OurWorldInData](https://ourworldindata.org/)
2. Use Ctrl + A to select all content from the page
3. Copy the content
